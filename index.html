Ethiopian Heritage

The Sacred Ritual of Buna.

In Ethiopia, coffee is never just a beverage — it is a communion of conversation, hospitality, and blessing.

Twice every day at Yohanan Coffee, we invite guests to sit together as green coffee beans are roasted over coals, frankincense smoke perfumes the room, and freshly ground coffee brews in our black clay jebena.

1
Abol (አቦል)

The first and boldest cup, rich in aroma and strength.

2
Tona (ቶና)

The second cup, smooth and deepening the conversation.

3
Baraka (በረካ)

The third cup, carrying blessings of peace and friendship.

🕒 Daily Ceremony Times: 10:00 AM & 4:00 PM Accompanied by warm fendisha (popcorn) and aromatic herbs.
